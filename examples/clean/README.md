# A clean set

A made-up export of a made-up help desk, Juniper Desk, for one week and a day, with `examples/sla.json`: urgent tickets
around the clock, every other priority from 09:00 to 12:30 and 13:30 to 17:30 at +01:00 on weekdays, with one holiday.
Every ticket meets its targets. One high ticket gets its first reply exactly at its target, one normal ticket has the
time it waited on the customer left out of its resolution time, one ticket raised on a Friday afternoon is answered on
Monday morning inside its target, and two tickets are still open, well inside their targets, as of the moment of the
examples.

The tool finds nothing in it.
